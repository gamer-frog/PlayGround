# 🌱 Hidroponía — Hydroponics Business Sandbox (3D Game, Desktop)

Grow, harvest and sell your way from a tiny inherited greenhouse to **Hydro Tycoon**.
A first-person 3D business sim: manage water, nutrients and pH, fight aphids and light
failures, fill restaurant contracts, ride fluctuating market prices, and expand to 8 racks.

**How to run:** serve the repo root statically (e.g. `python3 -m http.server`) and open it in a browser — no build step. Desktop + mouse & keyboard recommended.

**Controls:** `WASD` move · mouse look · `Shift` run · `E` interact/harvest/plant/repair · `F` water · `C` dose nutrients · `V` balance pH ($4) · `X` spray pesticide · `1–5` seed select · `B` shop · `Esc` pause · `M` mute

**Systems:** 5 crops · 8 expandable racks · daily price fluctuation · restaurant contracts · rack care (water/nutrients/pH/health) · random events (aphids, light failures) · 8 upgrades (LEDs, auto-dosers, solar…) · milestones · day cycle (06:00–22:00) · autosave + manual save (localStorage) · WebAudio synth SFX.
